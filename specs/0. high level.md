# Portfolio Card Splitter, high-level product spec

## Vision

Help two or more people share a credit card cleanly, assign each charge, park money elsewhere while the card is interest free, then settle with zero guesswork, all in a friendly, mobile-first app that reads like a polished portfolio piece.

## Users and access

- **Owner**, sets up the space, invites others, controls pot and account visibility, manages defaults
- **Member**, accepts invite, adds transactions, tags, reservations, payments, sees what the owner allows
- **Invite only**, no public sign up, first run bootstrap creates the first owner

## Problems to solve

- Whose charge is this, mine, yours, or shared, and by what portion
- We set money aside in a different place, show that it is reserved against this charge
- We made a partial payment, reflect it
- What is the running balance between us, who owes whom, by how much
- Give me a quick dashboard on my phone, what needs attention right now

## Product principles

- Simple first, powerful next, minimal fields, progressive disclosure, crisp copy
- Mobile first layouts, responsive components, accessible interactions
- Clear ownership and traceability, every change shows who did it and when
- Honest math, totals reconcile, no silent magic

## MVP, what it does

1. **Onboarding and access**

   - First run bootstrap, create the owner
   - Invite members by email, accept invite, sign in
   - Reset password via email

2. **Card accounts and cards**

   **CardAccounts** represent credit card accounts (issuer, name, last4, billing cycle, credit limit)

   **Cards** (optional) represent physical/virtual card numbers within an account:

   - Link a Card to a Member for "who swiped" tracking
   - Useful when multiple people have cards on the same account
   - Transactions can be tagged with a Card for filtering and insights
   - If no Cards are defined, transactions are simply attributed at the CardAccount level

   **Rules:**

   - Every transaction belongs to exactly one CardAccount
   - If only one CardAccount exists, it's selected by default
   - Cards are for attribution only; they don't affect splits or payments

3. **Dashboard, what matters now**

   - Recent transactions that are unallocated or unpaid
   - Outstanding totals per CardAccount, quick links to add, split, reserve, or pay
   - Pots running low, recent activity with who and when

4. **Transactions and splits**

   - Add a charge with date, merchant, description, amount, CardAccount
   - Transactions can be saved without allocations initially (marked "unallocated" on dashboard)
   - Mark it as mine, yours, or shared, percentages or fixed amounts, totals must reconcile before marking complete
   - Add tags and notes, quick edit later

5. **Merchants and tags**

   - Autocomplete merchants from prior entries, create on submit
   - Manage tags with colours, filter by tag

6. **Pots and reservations**

   - Track where money is parked, savings account, loan offset, cash, other
   - Mark pots as solo or shared, owner sets who can view or manage
   - Reserve amounts from a pot against a transaction, see reserved and remaining
   - Each member reserves against their own allocation separately (keeps it simple and traceable)

7. **Payments and transfers**

   - Record a payment from a pot to a transaction, partial or full
   - If payment amount differs from reservations, the system flags it for reconciliation (ensuring all funds are accounted for)
   - Move money between pots, or between a pot and cash, add notes
   - Payments on transactions reduce the outstanding total on the linked CardAccount automatically

8. **Balances and settlement**

   - See per person balances and the net who owes whom
   - Export a simple summary for manual settlement if needed

9. **Activity and audit**

   - Every meaningful change is logged with actor and time
   - Filter activity by entity or date, useful for quick reviews

10. **Changelog in the app**

- A screen that shows release notes, ties the working product to public releases

## Non-functional goals

- **Usability**, quick entry flows, keyboard and touch friendly, clear empty states
- **Accessibility**, readable contrast, visible focus, reduced motion respected
- **Performance**, fast first load on mobile, snappy navigation
- **Reliability**, edits are durable, collaboration feels near real time
- **Locale**, English Australia, Brisbane time, currency AUD, day-first dates

## Navigation outline

- Login, Invite Accept, Reset
- Dashboard
- Transactions, list and detail, filter by CardAccount
- Pots, list and detail, reserve side panel
- CardAccounts, simple settings, name, issuer, last4, billing cycle, credit limit display
- People, simple roster
- Ledger, balances and who owes whom
- Activity, chronological timeline
- Changelog

## Guardrails and rules

- A transaction must belong to exactly one CardAccount
- Transactions can be saved without allocations (marked "unallocated" on dashboard)
- Allocations must add up to the transaction total before the transaction is marked complete
- You cannot reserve more than is available in a pot, or more than your allocation amount
- Each member manages their own reservations against their allocation (no shared reservation tracking)
- Payments cannot exceed the transaction total, partials are allowed and tracked
- **Outstanding balance** per CardAccount = sum of all transaction amounts - sum of all payments to those transactions
- If a payment amount differs from reserved amounts, the system flags it for user reconciliation
- Payments reduce the outstanding total of the linked CardAccount automatically
- Solo pots can be hidden from others, shared pots are visible per the owner's settings
- CardAccounts with transactions cannot be deleted, archive instead

## Success signals

- Add a transaction in under ten seconds on a phone
- See a correct who owes whom value after a few edits, with no manual math
- Users can always tell why a total is what it is
- Recruiters can click through a demo, see a clear changelog, and understand the story in minutes

## Later, not in MVP

- **CSV import**, column mapping tied to a CardAccount, auto matching to payments or reservations, review queue
- **Statements**, close date, due date, minimum due, statement level payments that allocate across transactions
- Statement reconciliation, interest-free period reminders
- Notifications and webhooks
- Insights, category trends, monthly summaries, per card analytics
- Multi-household or multi-space support
- **Demo mode**, runs without a server for portfolio showcases

## Risks to watch

- Confusion between allocation, reservation, and payment, solved with naming, icons, and microcopy
- Pot privacy, solved with clear visibility controls and badges
- Merchant duplicates, solved with simple rename and merge tools later

That is the full product picture at a high level, ideas not tables, choices not code, ready to hand to planning or to shape into landing page copy.
